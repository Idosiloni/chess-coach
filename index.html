Find the move that lost you the game.

Load your chess.com games or paste a PGN. Stockfish reviews every move, picks the three mistakes worth learning from, and turns each one into a puzzle you can solve.

How do I get a PGN?
  1. Open the game on chess.com
  2. Click the share icon (or "Share / Export")
  3. Choose the PGN tab and copy it
